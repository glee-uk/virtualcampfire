# Risk Assessment: Campfire for up to 15 People

**Activity:** Campfire gathering  
**Number of participants:** Up to 15 people  
**Location:** Designated outdoor fire area  
**Persons at risk:** Participants, organisers, and members of the public nearby.

**Overall risk level:** Medium before control measures; Low to Medium when controls are followed.

## 1. Hazards and Control Measures

| Hazard | Possible Risks | Control Measures                                                                                                  |
|---|---|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Open flames and sparks | Burns or clothing catching fire | Keep a safe distance from the fire. Tie back long hair and avoid loose clothing. Supervise the fire at all times. |
| Fire spreading | Grass, trees, or nearby objects catching fire | Use a designated fire pit. Clear flammable materials from the area and check local fire restrictions.             |
| Children or vulnerable participants near the fire | Burns or accidental falls into the fire | Ensure close supervision and establish a clear safety boundary around the fire.                                   |
| Handling firewood and hot equipment | Cuts, splinters, and burns | Use suitable gloves and tools. Do not handle hot logs or embers with bare hands.                                  |
| Extinguishing the fire | Burns or fire reigniting | Keep water nearby. Fully extinguish the fire and check that ashes are cold before leaving.                        |
| Emergency situations | Delayed response to injury or spreading fire | Ensure a first-aid kit is available, identify an emergency contact, and keep access routes clear.                 |

## 2. Required Safety Arrangements

- One responsible adult must supervise the fire throughout the activity.
- Keep all 15 participants at a safe distance.
- Do not allow anyone to play with the fire.
- Do not use petrol or other flammable liquids to start or increase the fire.
- Do not light the fire during strong winds, drought restrictions, or where fires are prohibited.
- Ensure everyone knows where the first-aid kit and fire-extinguishing equipment are located.
- Extinguish the fire completely before the group leaves.

## 3. Risk Rating

| Assessment Stage | Risk Level |
|---|---|
| Before control measures | Medium to High |
| After control measures | Low to Medium |

## 4. Conclusion

The campfire can proceed provided the control measures are in place, weather conditions are suitable, and the fire is continuously supervised by a responsible person.
